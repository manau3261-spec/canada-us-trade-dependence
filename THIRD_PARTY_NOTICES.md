## U.S. steel pilot

- U.S. Census Bureau, *Merchandise Trade Imports*, January, April and May 2025 snapshots. The pilot includes derived sums of imports-for-consumption customs value and calculated duties. Credit the Census Bureau as the original source. The import ZIPs and their legacy executable installers stay in the historical archive. [Census product](https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/data/IMDB.html) · [Census reuse and citations](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/citation.html)
- U.S. International Trade Commission, *Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States*, 2025 Revision 4. The release includes the scoped line crosswalk and source references. The full schedule source file is kept separately. [USITC revision](https://www.usitc.gov/2025_hts_revision_4)
- U.S. Department of Commerce, Bureau of Industry and Security, 90 FR 11249, 5 March 2025, and subsequent official instruments cited in the timeline. Legal documents are referenced by official URL. For U.S. federal works, consult the statutory rule and applicable source notices. [Federal Register annex](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-03-05/html/2025-03598.htm) · [17 U.S.C. § 105](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t17/s105/)
