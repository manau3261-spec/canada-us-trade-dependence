# Statistical inference audit

## Fixed-effect support

The primary panel contains 1,454 cells. The 108 province–product, 154 product–year and 140 province–year indicator levels have **combined rank 368** because some levels are linearly dependent. The three slope columns add three independent directions, giving full rank 371 and 1,083 residual degrees of freedom. The weighted QR computation uses equal weights for the primary fit. The scaled post-projection slope condition number is **2.060**. The HHI standard deviation is 0.16126 before effects, 0.08556 after effects, and **0.08359** after effects and controls. All ten provinces have nonzero identifying variation for the conditional HHI slope. These are design diagnostics. Omitted trends or reverse causality can remain even when rank and support are adequate. [Preflight](../data/phase4b/preflight.json)

## Covariance and small-cluster bootstrap

The province CR1 covariance sums scores within province. The finite-sample adjustment counts the rank of the identified fixed effects and slopes. Student t reference tests use nine degrees of freedom. Overlapping one-year growth outcomes can share endpoint years. Province clustering allows unrestricted such within-province dependence, including across products. Common product–year mean shocks are absorbed. Residual cross-province dependence can still affect province inference. The two-way covariance adds province and product components and subtracts the province–product intersection component using the corresponding corrections. It uses nine degrees of freedom. The province–product-only sensitivity treats different cells as independent after controls and imposes a stronger assumption. For each tested HHI null, the restricted fit includes the remaining slopes and absorbed effects. The bootstrap applies one of six equal-probability Webb weights to each province’s restricted residual vector. The pseudo-outcome adds the weighted residuals to the restricted fitted value. Each draw is refit in the same full design and studentized with province CR1. The reported interval inverts the two-sided tests over the registered scan and further refines the two boundaries. There was a single bounded acceptance interval in the scanned range. The approximate Monte Carlo standard error is **0.00450** at the zero null and **0.00218** near a p-value of 0.05. The bootstrap relies on independent province clusters with a suitable disturbance model. Ten clusters and uneven leverage preclude treating its finite-sample uncertainty as exact. [Webb weights](https://doi.org/10.1111/caje.12661) · [Full diagnostic output](../data/phase4b/primary_diagnostics.json)

The efficient bootstrap projects group-specific restricted residuals once and uses the linear response of coefficients and scores to each vector of multipliers. An independent explicit-dummy statsmodels fit agreed within **6.44 × 10⁻¹⁵** for coefficients and **2.03 × 10⁻¹³** for covariance diagonals. The literal restricted bootstrap refits agreed within **9.33 × 10⁻¹⁵** in t statistics across 24 draws at each of two nulls. This checks the implementation on the actual regression input, including the absorbed effects and covariance correction. It does not establish statistical assumptions. ## Influence and limits

New Brunswick contributes **17.85%** of the fully partialled HHI sum of squares, followed by Newfoundland and Labrador at **16.29%** and Nova Scotia at **14.13%**. The inverse concentration of provincial leverage shares is **7.89** effective provinces as a descriptive support measure. The highest individual full-design hat diagonal is 0.613 in Prince Edward Island. Its omission and Nova Scotia’s each change the sign of the estimated slope. Those refits rebuild the full design and retain a broad confidence interval. Reporting the omission checks together with the two-way covariance and non-overlap exercises is warranted. A province-level WCR test does not solve cross-province dependence, and two-way clustering with ten and eleven marginal clusters is also imprecise. [Influence data](../data/phase4b/province_influence.csv) · [Influence figure](../figures/phase4b/en/03_province_influence.png)

**Statistical assessment.** The registered rank and numerical inference checks passed. The primary and alternative covariance methods agree that the data provide an imprecise estimate that spans zero. Destination-specific shocks, heterogeneous commodity prices, persistence and anticipatory portfolio adjustment remain substantive sources of endogeneity. No causal interpretation or out-of-sample forecasting claim follows from these tests.
