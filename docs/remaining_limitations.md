# Remaining limitations and issue register

Each item has one disposition. “Blocking” refers to the specific release or deployment scope in the last column.

| ID | Classification | Finding and disposition | Scope |

| B1 | Blocking | The owner must explicitly approve the audited candidate before it is made public. | Any public release |
| B2 | Blocking | Confirm author names, affiliations, acknowledgements and contribution attribution. Choose the licence or licences for original code, paper, figures and derived files, preserving the source-specific notices. | Public repository and papers |

| I1 | Important but non-blocking | Absolute nominal log movements do not identify conditional variance, real volatility or a causal effect. Concentration can respond to anticipated sales risks. Omitted province–product shocks can remain after the three fixed effects. The estimates and robustness signs are reported in full. | Scientific scope |
| I2 | Important but non-blocking | Ten province clusters and uneven partial leverage make finite-sample inference fragile. The bootstrap and alternate CR1 comparisons show the available precision, which is limited. | Scientific scope |
| I3 | Important but non-blocking | Strict HHI construction excludes cells with unresolved partner coverage, and positive-flow and lag floors select observed active portfolios. Territories and special transactions are outside Design C. Retain the exclusions ledger and population limits. | Scientific scope |
| I4 | Important but non-blocking | Published HHI and the strict reconstruction disagree beyond rounding for some national and regional observations. The archived 2022 evidence is consistent with a vintage explanation. Residual allocation and publisher inputs remain incompletely documented. Preserve both series. | Scientific scope |
| I5 | Important but non-blocking | The active 2020 annual and monthly tables have 228 differences outside rounding. Archived tables reconcile. The precise record-level reason remains unresolved. Use annual data for Design C and an explicitly separate monthly window. | Data interpretation |
